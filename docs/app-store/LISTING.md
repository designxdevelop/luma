**Description:**
Luma is an experimental app for controlling Govee lights from iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

- Power, brightness, color, and white temperature for each light, plus model-specific effects
- Rooms and search to find lights fast
- Scenes you create, edit, duplicate, and share as JSON
- Schedules and an authenticated webhook server for local automations
- Screen Sync: point the back camera at a TV or monitor and your lights follow its colors. Frames are reduced on the device and never saved or uploaded
- Apple Watch companion: toggle lights, dim with the Digital Crown, run scenes
- Direct LAN control where supported, or Govee cloud control with your own API key
- No account, no ads, no tracking
- Explore Demo mode with sample lights, no hardware needed

Good to know: iOS suspends apps in the background, so schedules, scenes, and the webhook server run only while Luma is open. Model support varies. Luma is open source (MIT) and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Govee.

## App Review notes
No sign-in is required. To review without Govee hardware or an API key, open the Lights tab and tap **Explore Demo**; it loads sample lights, scenes, and Screen Sync using a synthetic sample. The camera permission is used only by Screen Sync (Use Camera) to read a coarse color grid from a TV or monitor; frames are processed on the device and never stored or sent. Local Network permission is used to find and control Govee lights over their LAN protocol. Luma is not affiliated with Govee and uses no Govee logos or marks. The Apple Watch app relays commands to the iPhone app.
